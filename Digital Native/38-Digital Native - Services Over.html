What is your typical availability?
I typically book project-based work 4-6 weeks in advance. For advisory retainers, I maintain limited slots to ensure high responsiveness for existing clients. Contact me to check current quarter availability.
Do you work with in-house engineering teams?
Yes, the majority of my engagements involve augmenting or guiding existing in-house teams. I focus heavily on knowledge transfer and upskilling your current engineers rather than creating dependency.
Are you open to full-time roles?
I am currently operating strictly as an independent consultant and am not seeking full-time employment. However, I often take on interim leadership roles (3-6 months) to help establish technical direction while you hire a permanent lead.
What stack do you specialize in?
While I am framework-agnostic at an architectural level, my deepest expertise lies in the JavaScript/TypeScript ecosystem (React, Next.js, Node.js), cloud-native infrastructure (AWS, GCP), and high-performance WebGL rendering.
