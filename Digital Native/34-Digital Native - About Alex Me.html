I translate complex logic into human experiences.

I didn't start out wanting to build interfaces. I started wanting to understand how systems communicate. That curiosity led me from backend architecture to the precise point where humans interact with machines.

For me, a great interface isn't just a layer of paint; it's a structural necessity. It's the difference between a tool that frustrates and one that empowers. I believe in performance as a design principle and simplicity as the ultimate sophistication.

When I'm not obsessing over frame rates and component lifecycles, I'm usually exploring the intersection of AI and generative art, trying to find new ways to make the digital world feel a little more tactile.

Operating Principles.

Performance = UX

Speed is a feature. A 100ms delay is a broken promise to the user. Every architectural decision is weighed against its performance cost.

Radical Simplicity

Complexity is easy; simplicity is hard. I strive to remove the unnecessary so the essential may speak. Interfaces should fade into the background.

Empathy in Code

We write code for machines to execute, but for humans to maintain and use. Clear documentation and accessible markup are non-negotiable.
