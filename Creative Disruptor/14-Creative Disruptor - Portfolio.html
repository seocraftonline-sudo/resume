Nexus Alpha

Predictive Analytics Platform

Client

Nexus Financial Group

Role

Lead Product Designer

Tools & Skills

Figma React D3.js Prototyping

The Challenge

Nexus Financial required a complete overhaul of their legacy trading terminal. The existing system was dense, visually fatiguing, and slowed down decision-making for high-frequency traders. The challenge was to introduce a modern, generative UI system that could handle massive data density without sacrificing clarity or aesthetic refinement.

The Solution

We developed a 'controlled disruption' layout model. Instead of rigid grids, we utilized floating, glassmorphic panels that adapt to user focus. Key metrics were highlighted using stark color contrast (vibrant reds against deep charcoal) to draw the eye immediately to actionable data.
