I'm Aria Chen, a creative force who sees the world differently. For 12 years, I've been crafting experiences at the intersection of art, technology, and controlled disruption.

01 — Who I Am

The Story Behind the Work

I don't just design interfaces. I orchestrate experiences that make people stop, lean in, and feel something they didn't expect to feel.

My journey started in the world of architecture, where I learned that every space tells a story. I spent three years designing physical environments before realizing that digital spaces could be just as emotional, just as powerful, and infinitely more malleable.

The transition wasn't smooth. I taught myself to code at 2am in a cramped Brooklyn apartment, fueled by cold brew and the belief that the best designers understand their medium at the deepest level. That decision changed everything.

Today, I lead creative strategy for a major tech company while running my own studio on the side. I work with brands that aren't afraid to be different, with founders who understand that safe is the riskiest choice you can make.

My work has been featured on Awwwards, FWA, and Behance. But the real reward is when someone tells me they've never seen anything quite like what we created together.

03 — Core Beliefs

What I Stand For

These principles guide every decision, every pixel, every conversation.

Emotion First

Logic gets attention. Emotion gets remembered. I design for the feeling you can't quite name.

04 — Beyond the Screen

Life Outside Work

When I'm not designing, you'll find me hunting for mid-century brutalist furniture in Brooklyn warehouses. There's something about exposed concrete and raw materials that speaks to my design philosophy.

I also restore vintage synthesizers—Moogs, Korgs, and Roland classics. The mechanical nature of analog sound design mirrors digital design in ways most people don't see. Both require understanding complex systems to create simple, beautiful outputs.

I'm passionate about mentoring young designers, especially those from underrepresented backgrounds. I volunteer with Code2040 and teach weekend workshops on design thinking.

I believe the best work comes from a full life. My hobbies aren't distractions—they're research.
