Clarifications

Common inquiries regarding the process, expectations, and logistics of engagement.

Still have questions?

What is your typical availability for new projects?

I typically book engagements 1-2 months in advance to ensure dedicated focus on current clients. However, for rapid strategic consultations or urgent disruptive campaigns, expedited timelines can occasionally be accommodated depending on the scope.

Do you work with in-house development teams?

Yes. While I offer full-stack execution, I frequently operate as a strategic creative director alongside existing internal teams. In these scenarios, I provide the architectural vision, design systems, and technical oversight while your team handles implementation.

How are revisions handled during the creative process?

The process is highly collaborative, meaning there are rarely "big reveals" that require massive revisions. We align on strategy first, then move through iterative phases with built-in feedback loops. Specific package terms define the exact number of structured revision rounds.
