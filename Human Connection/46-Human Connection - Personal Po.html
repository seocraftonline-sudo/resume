Wellness 2023

Aura Wellness App

"How might we make daily habit tracking feel less like a chore and more like checking in with a supportive friend?"

Client

Aura Health Inc.

Role

Lead Product Designer

Timeline

4 Months

Platform

iOS & Android

The Challenge

Aura had a high download rate but struggled with day-7 retention. Users reported feeling overwhelmed by the sterile, data-heavy interface. The challenge was to redesign the core tracking loop to foster emotional connection and reduce cognitive load, all without losing the robust data capabilities power users loved.

The Approach

I began by conducting 15 deep-dive interviews with churned users. The insight was clear: people felt guilty when they missed a day, and the app's red warning colors amplified that guilt. We shifted the design language from "punitive tracking" to "gentle encouragement."

The Solution

The new interface utilizes organic shapes, a calming dark-green palette, and conversational microcopy. We replaced rigid streak counters with a "garden" visualization that grows even if you miss a day, provided you return.

  • Conversational Onboarding: Replaced static forms with a chat-like interface to build immediate rapport.
  • Fluid Micro-interactions: Added subtle, organic animations that respond softly to user touch, mimicking natural elements.

Outcomes

Increase in Day-7 Retention
-28%
Drop in Onboarding Abandonment
4.8
