How We Work Together

A transparent look at the journey from our first hello to the final handoff. No black boxes, just clear communication.

1

The Alignment Call

We start with a 45-minute casual conversation. I want to hear about your vision, your current roadblocks, and what success looks like to you. If we're a good fit, I'll follow up with a detailed proposal.

2

Deep Dive & Discovery

Once approved, we kick off. I immerse myself in your world—reviewing existing data, talking to your users, and mapping out the strategic foundation before a single pixel is pushed.

3

Iterative Creation

We work in transparent, weekly cycles. You'll see the messy middle. We'll review concepts, refine them based on feedback, and progressively increase fidelity until we have a polished solution.

4

Handoff & Support

Delivery isn't just sending a Figma link. I provide comprehensive documentation, walk your developers through the logic, and remain available for QA as they build out the designs.

Common Questions

Things people usually ask before we start.

Do you work with early-stage startups?

Yes, absolutely. I love the energy of early-stage teams. My strategic discovery service is specifically designed to help founders validate their ideas before committing to expensive development cycles.

What design tools do you use?

Figma is my primary tool for all UI/UX work and prototyping. For strategy and mapping, I often use FigJam or Miro. I'm also comfortable working directly in code (HTML/Tailwind) for rapid prototyping if needed.

How do you handle revisions?

I don't strictly cap revisions because we work iteratively. By reviewing progress weekly, there are no massive "reveals" that require sweeping changes. We pivot together as we learn more about the problem.
