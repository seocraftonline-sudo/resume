The story behind the work

More than just a designer.

If we were grabbing coffee right now, I wouldn't start by handing you my resume. I'd probably ask what you're reading or what problem is keeping you up at night.

I believe that the best design doesn't come from a vacuum or a perfectly optimized Figma file. It comes from understanding the messy, beautiful reality of how people actually live and work. My journey here wasn't a straight line—it was a series of curious detours that taught me how to listen, how to empathize, and how to build things that actually matter.

"Design is just the medium I use to connect with people."

The Journey So Far

Not just a list of titles, but the defining moments that shaped how I approach problems today.

The Pivot to Empathy

Transitioned from traditional graphic design to UX after realizing that making things look good wasn't enough. I wanted to understand why things worked.

2016

Joined Studio XYZ

First role purely focused on user research and wireframing.

2019

Lead Designer at HealthTech

Led a team of 4 to redesign a core patient portal used by 100k+ users.

Designing for Stakes

Working in healthcare taught me that bad design isn't just annoying; it can be dangerous. This is where my obsession with accessibility and clarity began.

Going Independent

Stepped away from agency life to work directly with founders. I wanted to be closer to the vision and have a more intimate relationship with the products I was building.

2022 - Present

Independent Consultant

Partnering with early-stage startups to build foundational design systems and culture.

What I believe in.

These aren't just corporate buzzwords. These are the non-negotiable principles that guide how I work, who I work with, and what I put out into the world.

Empathy over Ego

The best ideas rarely come from the loudest person in the room. They come from quiet observation and a genuine desire to understand someone else's struggle. I leave my ego at the door.

Organic Growth

Forcing metrics often breaks trust. I believe in designing systems that grow organically because they actually provide value, not because we manipulated a user into clicking a button.

Clarity is Kindness

Confusion is the enemy of good design. Whether it's a complex dashboard or a simple onboarding flow, my goal is always to reduce cognitive load and make the path clear.

Skin in the Game

I don't just hand over a Figma file and disappear. I care deeply about the execution, the business outcomes, and the long-term health of the product. Your success is my success.
