Lumina Wealth

Architecting a unified asset management platform managing over $1B in AUM. A complete reimagining of how high-net-worth individuals interact with complex financial data.

Client

Lumina Financial Group

Year

2023

Role

Lead Product Designer

The Challenge

Traditional wealth management platforms are notoriously dense, prioritizing data volume over cognitive clarity. Lumina needed a system that could handle immense complexity without overwhelming the user. The goal was to create an interface that felt as tailored and precise as the financial advice they provide.

The Process

We began by stripping away layers of legacy data visualization, focusing instead on progressive disclosure. By implementing a 'quiet design' framework, we ensured that primary metrics commanded attention while secondary data remained accessible but unobtrusive. Extensive prototyping and user testing refined the micro-interactions.
