Common Questions

What is your typical availability?

I typically book projects 4-6 weeks in advance. For smaller advisory roles or audits, I can sometimes accommodate within 2 weeks depending on current workload.

Do you handle implementation or just design?

I focus strictly on product strategy, UX architecture, and high-fidelity UI design. However, I provide meticulous documentation and work closely with your engineering team to ensure perfect implementation.

How do you handle revisions?

My process is highly collaborative, meaning there are rarely 'big reveals' that require massive revisions. We align on strategy and structure early, so visual execution is usually a smooth refinement process.
