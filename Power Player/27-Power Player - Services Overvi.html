Methodology

The Engagement Process

A disciplined, structured approach to defining scope, executing strategy, and measuring impact.

01

Initial Diagnostics & Alignment

We begin with a confidential briefing to understand the macro-level challenges. This phase involves reviewing existing documentation, identifying structural friction points, and determining if there is mutual fit for an engagement.

Output

A preliminary diagnostic report outlining the core issues and proposed strategic direction.

02

Scope & Architecture Design

Once aligned, we architect the specific interventions required. This involves defining precise deliverables, establishing timelines, mapping stakeholder involvement, and formalizing the commercial terms of the engagement.

Output

A comprehensive Statement of Work (SOW) detailing the engagement architecture.

03

Execution & Integration

The active phase of the engagement. We implement the agreed structural changes, working directly with the board or C-suite. This phase is characterized by rigorous oversight, regular progress reporting, and adaptive problem-solving.

04

Handoff & Institutionalization

Strategies are only valuable if they endure. The final phase focuses on embedding the new frameworks into the organization's DNA, training internal teams for continuity, and establishing long-term measurement metrics.

Engagement Parameters

Common queries regarding scope, availability, and operational mechanics.

What size organizations do you typically advise?

Engagements are typically focused on mid-market to enterprise-level organizations (revenues of $50M - $1B+), pre-IPO tech firms, and established financial institutions where structural complexity requires specialized oversight.

Do you work with internal teams or independently?

I operate primarily at the board and C-suite level but interface closely with internal execution teams (legal, finance, operations) to ensure strategic directives are practically implementable and culturally aligned.
