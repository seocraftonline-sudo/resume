The Journey

A narrative of strategic evolution, from early foundational roles to shaping global enterprise architecture.

The Foundation: Risk & Reality

1998 — 2005

My career began in the trenches of risk management at a Tier-1 investment bank during a period of unprecedented market volatility. It was here I learned that models are only as good as the assumptions behind them. I quickly transitioned from analyzing risk to advising on structural resilience, learning to identify the invisible fault lines in large organizations before they fractured.

The Pivot: Strategic Scaling

2006 — 2015

Recognizing that true value creation happens at the intersection of strategy and execution, I moved into management consulting, eventually leading the M&A integration practice at a global firm. During this decade, I oversaw the post-merger integration of over $40B in assets. The core lesson? Culture eats strategy for breakfast, but operational dysfunction eats them both. I developed proprietary frameworks for aligning disparate corporate cultures while maintaining operational momentum.

The Present: Boardroom Advisory

2016 — Present

Today, I operate exclusively at the board and C-suite level. I established Nexus Group to provide unfiltered, high-stakes advisory without the overhead of traditional consulting models. My current focus is guiding legacy institutions through digital transformation and advising high-growth tech firms on sustainable enterprise scaling. I sit on the boards of three publicly traded companies, providing governance and strategic oversight.

Core Philosophy

The non-negotiable principles that dictate how I operate, advise, and lead.

Unvarnished Truth

I am not hired to validate existing opinions. I am hired to identify blind spots and articulate the realities that internal teams are often too compromised to voice. Candor is my primary currency.

Execution Over Ideation

A brilliant strategy that cannot be executed is a failed strategy. Every framework I design is pressure-tested against the operational realities and cultural constraints of the organization.

Long-Term Viability

I refuse to optimize for short-term optics at the expense of long-term structural integrity. My recommendations are built to withstand market cycles, not just the next quarterly earnings call.
